What's new

1.0.69
I added a cap for Action and Expression Menu icons. On Upload walks the assembled menus and only resizes icons that are actually on those controls. 256, 128, or 64. If none are larger than the pick, I skip that step. Copies stay on the upload clone. An icon that is also on a material stays on that material; the menu gets its own copy.
Textures can write the same cap on the importer. That row starts off. I only list icons from the menus on the avatar in the scene, and I leave an icon that is also on a material alone. Menus that only show up after upload assembly are not in that list — use On Upload for those.
UI — On Upload: Cap Action / Expression Menu icons sits under Menus, after Contacts. It starts on at 256 when Apply on upload is ticked.
UI — Textures: Action / Expression Menu icons sits at the bottom of Max Size By Type. 256, 128, or 64. It starts off. A note under the type list says to use On Upload for menus that appear only after assembly.

1.0.68
I added Merge basic meshes on On Upload. It is off unless you turn it on. It combines always-visible MeshRenderer bits that share a parent — hair clips, glasses, that kind of thing. Extra renderer components come off; the objects stay. Toggles, transform animations, and anything another component still points at stay separate.
UI — On Upload: Merge basic meshes sits under Meshes, after Merge skinned meshes. It starts off.

1.0.67
I fixed On Upload stuffing extras onto the body when they still have their own bones. You would change the pants and the tail or the straps would jump. Those stay on their own bones now. I do not dump leftover weights onto Chest.
If another upload pass is still on the copy, I wait. I do not take those components off. EditorOnly objects that still have something on them stay. PhysBones on those leftovers stay too.

1.0.66
I added unused contacts to On Upload. It takes off contact senders and receivers that are disabled and never turn on. Enabled senders stay, so other avatars can still hit you. If a receiver still feeds a parameter into FX, menus, or Expression Parameters, I leave it. I also leave anything on your Ignore List, contact-system names, and anything else on the avatar that still points at a contact. You do not have to open the window for that default — Unity writes it when the project loads. Apply on upload stays off unless you already had it on.
UI — On Upload: Remove unused contacts sits under Contacts, after PhysBones. It starts on when Apply on upload is ticked.
UI — Profiles: the On Upload tab checkbox lists contacts.
